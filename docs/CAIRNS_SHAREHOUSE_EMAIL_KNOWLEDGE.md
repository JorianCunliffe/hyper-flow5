Source: information supplied by the operator on 25 September 2026. This is an approved static reference, not a live availability feed. Prices are AUD per week, all bills included. Website claims have not been independently refreshed.

## Included

- Electricity, water, air conditioning and unlimited high-speed WiFi.
- Basic bedding. An upgraded bedding pack can be hired for $40.
- Furnished room with lockable door, double/single/twin bed, study desk, wardrobe and ceiling fan. The bed configuration of each individual room must be confirmed separately.
- Shared kitchen, lounge with TV, two showers and two toilets per apartment.
- Bathrooms professionally cleaned weekly; whole apartment professionally cleaned fortnightly.
- Parking, bike racks and a security gate. A specific parking space is not guaranteed by this statement.
- The Martyn and Lady Loeven have a pool. Most houses have a BBQ; confirm for a particular house if needed.

Residents bring towels, toilet paper and washing powder, and contribute their share of cleaning products, bin bags and dishwashing liquid. Coin laundry costs $4 per use, except $2 at The Penthouse.

## Booking, holding and payment

- Rooms are held for 24 hours only. If payment is not received within 24 hours, the room is released to the next person. Do not imply that this automation has placed a hold.
- Booking deposit: $50, deducted from the arrival balance.
- After payment, email a screenshot of the deposit to info@cairns-sharehouse.com so staff can match it to the booking.
- Rent is paid in advance, usually at least 24 hours before moving in.
- Arrival balance: first two weeks' rent, last two weeks' rent, and a $160 security deposit, with the booking deposit deducted as applicable.
- Payment options: direct deposit, online credit card (2% fee), or cash at any Commonwealth Bank branch. Bank details and payment links are not supplied here and must not be invented.

## Move-in and move-out

- Move-in: 3:00pm sharp. An earlier arrival requires booking the previous night.
- Move-out: 10:00am. A later departure requires booking an extra night.
- Times are local Cairns time (Australia/Brisbane).

## Inspections

- Weekdays only.
- 80 Martyn St: 4:00pm.
- All other properties: 3:30pm.
- Staff confirmation is required before attending. These are usual inspection times, not evidence of an available or confirmed appointment on a particular date.

## Stay, notice and fees

- Minimum stay: 3–4 weeks. The exact minimum for a particular booking is not specified and requires confirmation.
- Maximum stay: 12 months; longer stays can be discussed.
- Give two weeks' notice before leaving. Without enough notice, no refund is provided under the supplied policy.
- Cancellation: $50 before paying the arrival balance, or $75 within seven days of arrival. Staff must clarify which fee applies where these conditions overlap.
- Room change administration fee: $150.
- Exit clean: $80 per bed, deducted from the security deposit.
- Remaining security deposit returned within one week of moving out, less damage deductions.

## House rules

- Residents must be at least 18. No children or pets under the supplied house policy.
- No smoking inside; smoking is allowed only in marked outdoor areas.
- Visitors must be booked in advance with contact details.
- Residents keep their room clean, wash their own linen weekly, clean up after themselves, and keep noise reasonable.
- Each resident receives a labelled fridge shelf and their own cupboard space.

## Drafting requirements

Address each substantive question across both subject and body. Use this reference for static facts and a fresh, successful availability result for room listings. Ask only for missing details relevant to the enquiry; do not substitute generic follow-up questions for known answers.
